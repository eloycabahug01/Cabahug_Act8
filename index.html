ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

MC // OVERWORLD ARCHIVE is a minimalist fan-made digital showcase dedicated to documenting the core heroes, guardians, and survivalists of the Minecraft universe. Designed with a dark pixel aesthetic, this platform serves as an interactive roster celebrating the iconic characters that define the Overworld experience.


From the creative mastery of Steve and the resourceful exploration of Alex to the unwavering protection offered by Iron Golems, each defender represents a vital aspect of survival. All assets and characters belong to Mojang Studios.
